WOODSTYLE — PRIVATE COLLECTION

Premium Liquid Glass edition: translucent, softly refractive interface panels in an espresso, ivory and champagne palette, detailed engraved 3D product views, and a quick switch to the original product photographs.

START
Open index.html in a browser, or upload the entire folder contents to your web hosting.
The site has no build step and no external JavaScript or font dependencies.

STRUCTURE
- index.html       Home page with interactive 3D product hero and atelier stage
- katalog.html     Product catalog with category filters and 3D product previews
- ueber-uns.html   About page
- kontakt.html     Contact links
- versand.html     Shipping and payment information
- impressum.html   Legal notice
- datenschutz.html Privacy policy
- css/style.css    Liquid-glass visual system, responsive layout and depth effects
- js/main.js       Mobile navigation, catalog filters, scroll effects and pointer depth
- js/wood-3d.js    Interactive 3D product scenes, wood grain, bevels and engraving
- images/          Existing local image assets

3D INTERACTION
- Drag the 3D objects with a mouse or finger; the rotation follows more smoothly and with lighter movement.
- Hold Alt or Shift and use the mouse wheel / trackpad scroll to rotate without changing the page scroll.
- Double-click a model (or press Home / 0 when focused) to return to the starting angle.
- The hero model also reacts to pointer movement on desktop.
- Use the small FOTO ↗ control to switch from a 3D view to the original 2D photograph; press ↻ 3D to return.
- Keyboard users can focus a model and rotate it with the arrow keys.
- Engraving includes finer wood grain, knots, pores, leaf veins, botanical details and more nuanced portrait linework.
- Models are drawn locally with Canvas 2D projection: no WebGL or external library is required.

LIQUID GLASS DESIGN
- Frosted glass navigation, translucent product cards, floating glass controls and layered champagne edge highlights.
- Dark smoked-glass 3D display stage and soft ivory glass panels on catalog and information pages.
- Backdrop blur includes readable opaque fallbacks on browsers without backdrop-filter support.
- Existing product photos remain available as a visual fallback if JavaScript is disabled.
- Motion is reduced automatically for visitors who prefer reduced motion.

CONTENT
All existing German copy, product prices, contact destinations and legal pages are retained.
